Chitral

Living Kalasha heritage, riverside forts, and the towering crest of Tirich Mir.

Plan this Trip

Home of Kalash Valleys and Tirich Mir.

Tucked deep inside the Hindu Kush range in northwestern Pakistan, Chitral blends rich cultural traditions with untamed alpine drama. Accessible year-round via the Lowari Tunnel, the valley stretches from Chitral Town’s historic fort to the vibrant Kalasha villages and the sky-high polo meadows of Shandur Pass.

Elevation 1,517 m (Town) – 7,708 m (Tirich Mir)
Access Lowari Tunnel (N-45) & Chitral Airport (CJL)
Famous For Kalasha Culture, Markhor & Shandur Polo

Unforgettable experiences around Chitral

  • ◆ Visit Bumburet, Rumbur, and Birir in the Kalash Valleys.
  • ◆ Explore the riverside ramparts of Chitral Fort and Shahi Mosque.
  • ◆ Drive up to Shandur Pass, the world’s highest polo ground.
  • ◆ Spot rare Kashmir markhor in Chitral Gol National Park.
  • ◆ Relax in natural geothermal pools at Garam Chashma hot springs.
  • ◆ Shop for handwoven Chitrali wool caps (pakol) and shawls in Shahi Bazaar.

Seasonal guide for visiting Chitral

Spring & Joshi (April – May)

Warm sunny days, blooming fruit orchards, and the joyful Kalash Joshi spring festival in mid-May.

Summer Peak (June – September)

Ideal for Shandur Pass, Qaqlasht meadows, and Uchal harvest celebrations in August.

Autumn & Winter (Oct – Dec)

Crisp golden valleys in October; snow blankets the peaks in December during the Chaumos winter festival.
