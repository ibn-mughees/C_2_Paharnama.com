Gilgit

Where the Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindu Kush mountain ranges converge.

Plan this Trip

Gateway to Hunza, Naltar and Fairy Meadows.

Nestled at 1,500 meters along the roaring Gilgit River, Gilgit is the historic Silk Road hub and administrative heart of Gilgit-Baltistan. From emerald pine valleys to dramatic 8,000-meter peak approaches, it serves as the ideal base for exploring northern Pakistan.

Elevation 1,500 m (4,921 ft)
Access KKH Highway & Gilgit Airport (GIL)
Famous For Naltar Lakes, Nanga Parbat & Polo

Unforgettable experiences around Gilgit

  • ◆ Take a 4x4 jeep safari up to Naltar Valley to see Satrangi and Blue Lakes.
  • ◆ Trek through pine forests to Fairy Meadows facing Nanga Parbat.
  • ◆ Visit the 7th-century Kargah Buddha rock relief and monastery ruins.
  • ◆ Catch a traditional freestyle polo match at Shahi Polo Ground.
  • ◆ Stand at the Three Mountain Ranges Junction near Jaglot.
  • ◆ Browse dried apricots, gemstones, and handicrafts in Raja Bazaar.

Seasonal guide for visiting Gilgit

Spring (March – April)

Apricot and cherry blossoms paint the valley white and pink with mild daytime temperatures (12°C – 20°C).

Summer Peak (May – September)

All high passes, Naltar Lakes, and Fairy Meadows trails are fully open with crisp mountain mornings.

Autumn (October – November)

Golden poplars and clear blue skies offer peak photography weather before winter snowfall arrives.
