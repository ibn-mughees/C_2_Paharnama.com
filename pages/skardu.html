Skardu

Glacial lakes, cold desert dunes, and the grand gateway to the Karakoram giants.

Plan this Trip

Base for K2, high lakes and the Deosai Plains.

Set in a wide sandy basin where the Indus and Shigar rivers meet at 2,228 meters, Skardu is the capital of Baltistan and the launchpad for expeditions to K2, Broad Peak, and Concordia. Beyond mountaineering, it rewards travellers with serene mirror lakes, centuries-old royal forts, and high-altitude plateaus.

Elevation 2,228 m (7,310 ft)
Access Skardu Int’l Airport (KDU) & Jaglot Road
Famous For Deosai, Shigar Fort & K2 Base Camp

Unforgettable experiences around Skardu

  • ◆ Cross the wildflower meadows of Deosai National Park on a 4x4 safari.
  • ◆ Tour the restored 17th-century heritage palace at Shigar Fort.
  • ◆ Photograph Nanga Parbat reflected in Sheosar Lake.
  • ◆ Ride ATVs or watch sunset across the Sarfaranga Cold Desert dunes.
  • ◆ Paddle wooden boats at Upper Kachura and Shangrila Resort lakes.
  • ◆ Hike up to historic Kharpocho Fort overlooking the Indus basin.

Seasonal guide for visiting Skardu

Spring (April – May)

Vibrant apricot blossoms transform Shigar and Skardu valleys; valley roads stay comfortable and uncrowded.

Summer Peak (June – September)

Best window for Deosai Plains, Sheosar Lake, and Karakoram treks once high snow passes thaw.

Autumn (October – November)

Amber foliage lines Lower & Upper Kachura lakes with crisp air and dramatic mountain visibility.
